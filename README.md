## 🏢 SaaS / Commercial Platforms

The cloud billing and chargeback market spans **native cloud provider tools** (AWS Billing Conductor) that generate pro forma bills for customized showback/chargeback, and **specialized FinOps platforms** that charge as a percentage of managed spend or per-resource fees. **AWS Billing Conductor** is **free for billing transfer users** but charges standard billing groups; it generates **pro forma data** that differs from the actual AWS bill and doesn't reflect real charges due to AWS . **CloudZero** differentiates through **unit economics** — measuring cost per customer, per team, or per feature . **Finout** provides **amortized cost calculations** for commitment-based billing, spreading Datadog commitment costs across the month for accurate daily breakdowns . **Harness CCM** offers a **Free Forever tier** for organizations under **$250K/year cloud spend**, with Enterprise SKUs for Commitment Orchestrator, Cluster Orchestrator, and AutoStopping . **Apptio Cloudability** (IBM) allocates **100% of cloud costs including containers, support, and shared services**, with ML-based forecasting and budget tracking .

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects

*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟

- **[Chitragupta](https://github.com/waliaabhishek/chitragupta)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/waliaabhishek/chitragupta?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/waliaabhishek/chitragupta/stargazers)  
  **Open-source chargeback engine framework**, open-source. **Named after the Hindu deity who maintains a complete record of every being's actions** — the divine accountant. **Tracks exactly who used what and how much it cost** . Brings together **billing, resource inventory, and usage data** across Confluent Cloud, self-managed Kafka, and Prometheus-instrumented systems. **v2 is a complete rewrite** with plugin architecture, multi-tenancy, FastAPI, proper storage layer, emitter framework, and docs site . **Demo mode runs without credentials** — `git clone` and `./demo --showcase` . 🏛️

- **[Kubecost (Open Source Core)](https://github.com/kubecost/cost-analyzer-helm-chart)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/kubecost/cost-analyzer-helm-chart?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/kubecost/cost-analyzer-helm-chart/stargazers)  
  **Kubernetes cost monitoring and optimization**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Free tier provides unlimited clusters with $100K spend cap over 30 days** in v3.0 . **Amazon EKS-optimized bundle** is free with no spend cap and integrates with AWS billing APIs for accurate pricing including **Savings Plans, Reserved Instances, and enterprise discounts** . ETL feature aggregates metrics for namespace-level, pod-level, and deployment-level visibility . 💰

- **[OpenCost](https://github.com/opencost/opencost)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/opencost/opencost?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/opencost/opencost/stargazers)  
  **Open-source cost monitoring for Kubernetes and cloud spend**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Originally developed and open-sourced by Kubecost** . Real-time cost allocation by cluster, node, namespace, controller, service, or pod. **Multi-cloud monitoring for AWS, Azure, GCP** with dynamic on-demand pricing from cloud billing APIs. **MCP server built into Helm chart** (opt-in, port 8081) for AI agent access to allocation, asset, and cloud cost queries. 🌱

- **[Kubernetes Metering Operator](https://github.com/kube-reporting/metering-operator)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/kube-reporting/metering-operator?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/kube-reporting/metering-operator/stargazers)  
  **Kubernetes chargeback and usage reporting**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **338 stars** . **Collects metrics and other information about what's happening in a Kubernetes cluster** and provides a way to create reports on the collected data . **Enables chargeback by namespace, pod, or label** — the foundation for Kubernetes cost allocation. 📊

- **[ccloud-chargeback-helper](https://github.com/waliaabhishek/ccloud-chargeback-helper)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/waliaabhishek/ccloud-chargeback-helper?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/waliaabhishek/ccloud-chargeback-helper/stargazers)  
  **Confluent Cloud Billing Chargeback Helper**, open-source. **32 stars** . Python-based tool for **allocating Confluent Cloud costs to teams and service accounts** . **Complements Chitragupta** for Kafka-specific chargeback scenarios. 🐍

- **[Cloud Custodian](https://github.com/cloud-custodian/cloud-custodian)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/cloud-custodian/cloud-custodian?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/cloud-custodian/cloud-custodian/stargazers)  
  **Rules engine for cloud security, cost optimization, and governance**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **~5k+ stars**. YAML-based policies for AWS, Azure, GCP. **Automated remediation**: tag enforcement (critical for chargeback allocation), idle resource cleanup, rightsizing, and compliance. **The most mature open-source cloud governance engine** — tag enforcement is the prerequisite for accurate chargeback. 🛡️

- **[Infracost](https://github.com/infracost/infracost)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/infracost/infracost?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/infracost/infracost/stargazers)  
  **Cloud cost estimates for Terraform in pull requests**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **~10k+ stars**. **Shift-left FinOps** — shows cost impact of infrastructure changes before deployment. Supports AWS, Azure, GCP, and **1,000+ resources**. CLI, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, and VS Code extension. **The standard for preventing cost regressions in CI/CD** . 💰

- **[Komiser](https://github.com/tailwarden/komiser)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/tailwarden/komiser?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/tailwarden/komiser/stargazers)  
  **Cloud resource visibility and cost optimization**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **~4k+ stars**. **Multi-cloud dashboard for AWS, Azure, GCP, DigitalOcean, and Kubernetes**. Detects idle resources, orphaned volumes, and untagged assets. **Self-hosted or Komiser Cloud**. Provides the visibility needed to act on optimization opportunities . 🔍

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️
- **AWS Billing Conductor pro forma data is not your actual AWS bill** — it's a customized view for showback/chargeback that differs from real charges due to AWS. Billing transfer groups are free; standard billing groups are charged .
- **Harness CCM Free Forever** limits cloud spend to **$250K/year** with **2 Kubernetes clusters** and **30-day data visibility**. Organizations exceeding these limits must upgrade to Enterprise SKUs .
- **Chitragupta's demo mode** uses synthetic data — no provider API keys required. Connect your own Confluent Cloud, self-managed Kafka, or Prometheus-instrumented systems for production use .
- Open-source solutions (Chitragupta, Kubecost, OpenCost) provide self-hosted ownership and transparency, but enterprise-grade SLA guarantees, multi-cloud cost allocation at scale, and vendor support remain primarily commercial offerings. **Always validate chargeback data against native cloud billing consoles** before issuing internal invoices. 💰
